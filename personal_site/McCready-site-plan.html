Overview

Purpose

The purpose of the site is to give information about the prestigious Academy of Super Smash Bros (A of SSB) to prospective students and parents. It is also for general info about the school's calendar and announcements

Audience

Our targeted audience for this site is prospective students and the parents of prospective students who wish to pursure a career in being a Smash Bro, as well as current students looking for information on school events and bell schedules.
